Why it doesn't betray you

Original = gospel

The rewrite is injected as an advisory contract. On any conflict, your original prompt wins. The daemon has seen what happens otherwise — it is what happens otherwise.

Proof-carrying rewrites

Structured JSON only: every explicit requirement backed by a source_quote — a verbatim substring of your prompt. Inferences are quarantined as non-binding.

Deterministic gates

Pure bash+jq review: schema check, quote-substring check, length ceiling. Any failure → your original passes untouched. ICE doesn't negotiate.

Injection containment

The forge call is non-agentic: --safe-mode --tools "". Pasted logs in your prompt can't hijack a daemon that has no hands.

Pass-through discipline

Already-clear prompts are left alone. Re-chroming preem is gonk vandalism.

Fail-open, always

Timeout, API error, malformed output — every failure path delivers your original prompt unchanged, with a visible warning. No prompt left behind.
